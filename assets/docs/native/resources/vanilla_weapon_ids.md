# Vanilla weapon ids

These are the **inventory ids** of vanilla weapons - the filename ids
that the inventory menu, shops, and quest rewards use. They are **not**
the same as a weapon's `obj_id` (the model/behaviour slot). For the
obj_id rules, the vanilla obj_id list, and the wpmask reservations see
[weapon_obj_ids.md](weapon_obj_ids.md).

Your own weapon needs an id between **1000 and 1999** as well - outside
that range the game does not treat it as a weapon. Pick one of the free
numbers in the range; the 39 listed below are taken.
